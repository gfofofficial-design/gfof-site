    Data handling and lookup limits

    The address goes to our hosting service and the configured Solana RPC provider (Helius when enabled, otherwise Solana’s public RPC). Token mints go to Jupiter for reference prices. Choosing an asset’s Lens link opens a separate mint lookup. This page holds the response in memory. Service providers may retain operational logs.

    The lookup asks for no signature or transfer. It reads native SOL and token balances returned by the original Token Program and Token-2022. Ownership, spendability and eligibility require separate verification.

    Source fields and limits

    One finalized Solana getMultipleAccounts response supplies the same slot for all seven accounts. The read checks the mint, three Streamflow contracts and three escrows. The endpoint can serve a cached response for up to 30 seconds.

    For each active schedule, the displayed amount is the lower of the observed escrow balance and the remaining principal after recorded withdrawals. Canceled schedules contribute zero. The percentage uses verified minted supply for this mint.

    Coverage is limited to these three contracts. A full cliff is the time scheduled tokens become eligible for withdrawal. Withdrawal is recorded separately. This read does not establish staking rewards or a funded reward pool.

    A little help with your dashboard.

    Do I need to sign in or connect a wallet?

    No. You can paste a public Solana wallet address to read its balances without signing in. The optional Phantom button asks to share your public address; this preview requests no signature or transaction. Never enter a seed phrase or private key.

    Open the wallet viewer →

    Why does a token have no price?

    Some returned tokens have no usable reference price. Their quantities still appear in the wallet table, but they are excluded from the priced subtotal. A missing price does not mean a token is worth zero. The subtotal may cover only part of the address’s holdings.

    See all returned token quantities →

    Start with the Alliance guide to explore public tools, the fictional community map and proposed challenge formats. Live team enrollment and competition entries remain closed. Signing in or trying a demo does not enroll you.

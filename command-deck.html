Wallet radar

READ ONLY

Know what is in an address at a glance. No sign-in is needed for this public-address viewer.

Copy your public Solana address from your wallet’s Receive screen, then choose Read / refresh balances. Enter a wallet address here; use Dossier Lens to inspect a token mint.

Viewing sends this public address to our hosting service and Solana's public RPC. Token mint addresses are sent to Jupiter for reference prices. Each token’s Lens link opens Dossier with that mint address for a separate structure lookup. This viewer does not save wallet data to browser storage or an account. Service providers may retain operational logs. No signature or token transfer is requested. Never enter a seed phrase or private key.

What this wallet view includes

Native SOL and nonzero token balances, aggregated by mint across the original Token Program and Token-2022. Unknown tokens show their mint address; their names are not verified. This view does not establish ownership, spendability or staking eligibility. Provider-held, locked or staked positions are not included.

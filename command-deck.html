What this wallet view includes

Native SOL and nonzero token balances, aggregated by mint across the original Token Program and Token-2022. Unknown tokens show their mint address; their names are not verified. This view does not establish ownership, spendability or staking eligibility. Provider-held, locked or staked positions are not included. Reference values cover supported prices only; historical charts remain unconnected.

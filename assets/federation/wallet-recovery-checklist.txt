FEDERATION LEARN
PRIVATE WALLET RECOVERY CHECKLIST

Use a separate copy for your own records. Store it privately. Keep wallet
recovery phrases, keys, passwords, PINs, and backup shares in their own secure
storage.

1. List account types.
   For each record, note whether it is a self-custody wallet, exchange account,
   or project multisig. Record the supported network and the official provider.

2. Know who can act.
   Record the relevant roles, such as wallet owner, project signer, or estate
   representative, and how an authorized person can find the private plan.
   Make sure that person knows which provider or adviser to contact.

3. Record the recovery process.
   Note the official recovery guide and where an authorized person can find
   instructions. Check whether separately imported accounts need their own
   backups. For project accounts, record approval thresholds and delays.

4. Rehearse interruptions.
   Walk through a lost device and a missed reminder using example data. If you
   have a timed plan, check who can pause it, which channel they use, and how
   they confirm the pause before a deadline.

5. Keep it current.
   Review the plan after changing a device, signer, provider, or backup.

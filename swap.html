A LITTLE HELP GETTING STARTED

Understand the preview.

Is this a live swap?

No. This page can request indicative quotes only. An optional wallet connection displays your address only. It never requests a signature, creates a transaction or submits a trade. Federation sign-in is not needed for quote checking.

What do price impact, slippage and minimum output mean?

Price impact is the provider’s estimate of the price effect of the quoted trade. Slippage tolerance describes the permitted price movement for a trade, and minimum output is the provider’s reported lower output threshold. These figures can change. This quote preview creates no transaction, so it enforces none of those protections.

What if a quote is unavailable?

A provider may be unavailable, rate-limited or unable to find a route for that amount. This preview cannot distinguish every cause and will not invent a price. An unavailable check does not prove the token cannot trade elsewhere.

What if my work computer blocks the quote or wallet?

Quotes do not require a wallet. If your browser reports a certificate warning or your managed computer blocks an extension, leave those protections in place. Try later on your personal device or ask your IT team. The page cannot distinguish every network or browser restriction. Never install a wallet or change company security settings just to test this preview.

Does a quote mean there is enough liquidity?

It shows that the provider returned a route for that amount at that moment. It does not guarantee execution, a future price or acceptable price impact. Both directions and different trade sizes still need review before trading opens.

What data does a quote check share?

Jupiter receives the chosen token pair, amount and ordinary network information such as your IP address, and may retain operational logs. No wallet address or Federation account is included. This page stores no quote history; Clear removes the displayed result, but does not delete provider logs.

Will GFOF hold my tokens or private keys?

This preview can request permission to see your wallet address. It cannot hold your tokens or private keys. The proposed live version would use your own wallet for each trade approval. Never enter a seed phrase, private key or sign-in code on this page.

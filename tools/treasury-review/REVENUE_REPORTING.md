# Treasury revenue publication

The reporting interface published through PR182 includes reviewed monthly statements, asset/service filters, exact integer calculations, proportional monthly net bars and a matching accessible evidence table. No automatic payment ingestion or fee collection is enabled. Current public statement data is empty, not verified zero. No synthetic receipt is published.

When real fees begin, reconcile each completed UTC calendar month by source and asset. Add one row per month/source/asset in assets/federation/treasury-revenue-data.mjs with the exact fields enforced by treasury-revenue-core.mjs. Supported assets: SOL (9 decimals), native USDC (6). Amount strings are integer base units; no decimal/floating-point money. Sources: swap and other. Source-specific names/pricing/collection evidence for any new paid service must also be disclosed; extend the registry rather than silently lumping unrelated products together. A completed month can have an explicit reconciled zero; an omitted month is unreported.

Publish a reviewed /record/<slug>.json or .html statement with reporting scope, UTC dates, reconciliation date, collection addresses/payment method, matching public transaction or redacted payment references and gross/refund/collection-cost reconciliation. Do not publish customer identities, invoice secrets, tokens or private billing exports. Audit a receipt once: internal transfers, contributions, donations, trade volume and token appreciation are not service fees. Costs should cover collection costs only; operating expenses and profit are separate.

ReconciledOn must be a real date in a later month. The chart groups reported months only, uses one asset at a time, and permits negative net receipts. It does not verify the external evidence or completeness itself: review the linked record and deductions before publication. Corrections need a dated explanation preserving the original evidence, not silent historical replacement. Confirm actual fee activation and update the static service-status card when releasing live collection; adding a statement does not enable trading or charging.

Run node --test tests/treasury-revenue.test.mjs tests/static-publish.test.mjs and the static build; review a bounded synthetic fixture locally for new render behavior. Keep fixtures out of public statement data. Existing accounts, backup and Alliance gates are unaffected. PR173 was incorporated into the published PR182 release.

Before publication, build the static artifact and run `node tools/treasury-review/check-publication.mjs`. CI runs the same check after the build. It refuses future reconciliation dates and missing, empty or non-file evidence in the built artifact. Changes under `record/` also trigger this workflow, so removing a referenced statement is checked. This confirms local publication structure only: it does not verify receipts, evidence contents, fee activation or completeness. Fixtures belong in temporary test folders, never the public record.

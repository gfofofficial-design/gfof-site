# Wallet Recovery Drill v1

November 25, 2026 · Federation Learn

People practice three decisions that commonly involve different recovery paths. The example cases cover a lost self-custody device, an owner missing a scheduled check-in, and a family following a custodial provider’s estate process. The third case focuses on finding the authorized person and the official channel.

- Each case has two decisions. Feedback explains the next action and why a tempting option would fail. A wrong answer keeps the current step available. The missed check-in case shows the example plan’s rules in context.
- The practice uses fictional facts and native controls. The page starts with the first case, and people can choose any case in their own order. Changing cases starts that practice from the beginning.
- Sources appear with their cases. The direct guides and a blank downloadable checklist work if the interactive module cannot load.
- The checklist is for recording account types, responsible roles, authorized instructions and rehearsal dates in a private place. It keeps credentials and access secrets outside the plan.
- The page runs on first-party static assets. Its inputs are limited to choosing sample answers, with no background connections or saved progress. The case outcomes describe decisions in the simulation.

Acceptance covers every correct path, wrong answers and retry, switching cases, missing answers, keyboard focus, small viewports, no-JavaScript access to the guides, and the restrictions on submissions and connections. Publishing the lesson does not grant credentials, enable member access or affect any custodial system.

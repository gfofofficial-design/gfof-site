The Federation has
a Second Division. FEDERATION CAPITAL COMMAND

What you are reading is a provisional document. No product. No timeline. No allocation form. The Federation does not promise faster than it can ship.

The page exists because the registry name was reserved before any announcement could be made — exactly the same discipline applied to the token contract, the corrections log, and the lending spec. Reservation is not a commitment to ship. It is a commitment to ship correctly when the time arrives.

The Galactic Federation of Finance was always more than a token. The name implies a federation — a coalition of mandates — and a federation cannot be a single product. It is a structure under which separate mandates operate, each with its own discipline.

The current operational division is the Civilian Corps — what you have been seeing on the live channels. The token, the community, the transparency infrastructure, the corrections log, the research log. This is Division 01.

A second division is in design. Its working name is Federation Capital Command (FCC). Its mandate is institutional: tokenized real-world assets, autonomous capital strategies, restaking-secured underwriting. It will operate under separate legal structure, with separate disclosures, with separate counsel. It will not ship until the Civilian Corps milestones — bond, Raydium migration, Realms governance, Streamflow staking — have been operationally complete.

The Commanding Officer of FCC will be Commodore V. Helix. Helix's voice is austere, terminal-driven, briefing-format — the operational counterpart to Admiral Voss's recruiting voice. Where the Admiral addresses Recruits, Helix addresses Allocators. Both serve the same Federation.

None of this is announced publicly. None of it appears on the homepage roadmap. The corrections log makes no commitment to a date. That is the discipline of the document you are reading: it exists, but it does not promise.

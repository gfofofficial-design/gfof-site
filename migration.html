GFOF / Migration preparation

Preparing our next chapter.
Keeping holders in the process.

We are preparing to move GFOF to a platform that better fits what the Federation is building. The move is intended to give our tools, planned holder programs and the Federation’s long-term development a better foundation. The destination platform will be announced later. The plan is a 1:1 exchange by token count: one accepted old GFOF token for one replacement GFOF token.

Early transfers are accepted now.

The main deadline is November 4, 2026; extended ordinary intake runs through January 5, 2027. Dates use America/Chicago. The replacement contract address will be announced later.

How the planned exchange works

01 / Check the details

Verify the token and wallet.

The existing token mint and Treasury receiving wallet are listed above. The main deadline is November 4, 2026, with extended ordinary intake through January 5, 2027. Replacement-token details will follow through our official website and socials.

02 / Old tokens received

Transfer and record.

Holders who are ready can now send existing GFOF to the designated Treasury wallet. We record the holder, sending wallet, exact amount received and transaction reference.

03 / Replacement distribution

Return the same token count.

We plan to create the replacement token after most old-token deposits are in Treasury. Replacement distribution follows once the required new-token inventory is secured and accepted deposits are reconciled for 1:1 replacement. We record each payout and confirm the receiving wallet with the holder if it changes.

Time to participate

Participation begins October 5, 2026. The main deadline is November 4, 2026—30 days from that date. Dates use America/Chicago.

3 months total

Extended intake window

Extended ordinary intake remains open through January 5, 2027: three calendar months total from October 5, including the first 30 days. Dates use America/Chicago.

Locked holders stay on the register.

Our default is to wait for existing locks to unlock under their original terms. Registered locked allocations will be tracked separately for later review, including unlocks after the ordinary three-month intake window. They are not automatically forfeited at that cutoff.

Any alternative arrangement would need confirmation before being announced. We are not promising early unlocks or replacing still-locked tokens with an immediate liquid allocation.

A simple, private holder record.

We will track who transferred, how much was received, what replacement amount is owed and whether it has been sent. Transaction references help prevent double counting. Holder names and individual migration records will stay private.

The 1:1 ratio refers to token quantity. It does not promise an equal market price or value. Final eligibility and exception terms will be published with the opening notice.

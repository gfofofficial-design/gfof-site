A 30-credit reserve covers this particular repair. It is not a universal savings target, and a bigger emergency could require more.

A reserve gives you options when something unexpected happens. Using it for an emergency is its purpose; rebuilding it can be a later goal. If saving is difficult, that is a constraint to plan around, not a personal failure.

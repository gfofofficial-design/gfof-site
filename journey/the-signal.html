Step 2 of 3 · Look beneath the message

What would you check?

Open any clue. These are fictional findings inside the story. You can decide at any time.

The promise: how does 40 become 80?

The sender repeats “guaranteed” but provides no explanation of where the extra credits come from or what could go wrong.

A promised profit is not evidence that the money exists.

The sender: ask Command through a known channel

You open the crew's existing Command directory—not the contact supplied in the message. Command confirms it sent no such offer. The sender copied its identity.

Familiar branding and a famous name are not proof of authorization.

The crowd: examine the glowing reviews

The screenshots are supplied by the sender. You cannot independently confirm the people, payments, or withdrawals.

Unverified reviews do not prove fraud by themselves, but they do not establish trust either.

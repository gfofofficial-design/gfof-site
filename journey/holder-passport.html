Connected wallet

GFOF in this wallet

This page sends your public wallet address to the Federation site's read-only balance endpoint, which queries a Solana mainnet RPC. The page does not save your address or balance; the site host and RPC provider process the lookup. “Clear wallet view” clears this page only; use your wallet settings to revoke a site connection. This display is not a secure access credential or a snapshot of all your holdings. Never share a seed phrase. A balance does not grant a reward, yield, beta place or financial right.

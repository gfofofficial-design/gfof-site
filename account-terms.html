FEDERATION // ACCOUNT USE TERMS

The account’s scope.

Public account registration is not open. These terms describe the initial Federation account service being prepared.

Last updated October 20, 2026. The account service is operated personally by the Federation’s founder. For questions about these terms, contact gfofofficial@gmail.com.

What the account provides

The initial account service is being prepared for email-code sign-in and Google sign-in where enabled. Available methods are shown on the account page. Public registration remains closed. The Command Deck provides explicit public-wallet balance reads, supported reference prices, current-mint lock observations and a calculator for proposed staking terms.

Journey progress stays in your browser. Sign-in does not synchronize that progress, establish ownership of a wallet, or save wallet addresses and observations to your account. Dossier has its own service scope; Federation sign-in does not save or unlock its reports.

Your choices and permissions

Use a sign-in identity that you control and are permitted to use. Protect your access and follow the identity provider’s account requirements. Do not impersonate another person, attempt unauthorized access, or interfere with the service or its usage limits.

Phantom’s optional public-address permission is separate from account sign-in. The dashboard requests no message or transaction signature. Permissions and terms for third-party services are handled directly with those providers.

Readings and availability

Balances, prices and lock observations can be incomplete, delayed or unavailable. Review each reading’s date, source and stated limits before relying on it. Displayed token quantities and reference prices do not establish asset ownership or eligibility.

The service is developing. Features can change, be paused or become unavailable. Account sign-in does not open a staking pool, lending service, trading facility or permission to move funds. Proposed terms are subject to their own review and launch process.

Your information and closing an account

The account privacy notice explains sign-in data, cookies, device storage, public-wallet requests and recovery records.

To request deletion, contact gfofofficial@gmail.com. We verify account control before removing active records and explain the outcome and known limits. Signing out or stopping use does not delete the provider account record. Local browser progress and public blockchain records are separate.

First pilot proposal: four projects, one Creative Clash

Proposed duration: two weeks after a separately announced start. Four mutually approved projects, with up to three eligible entries each. No projects, dates, judges or prize commitments are confirmed.

  1. One shared brief. Publish the deliverable, permitted tools and AI-use disclosure, originality/rights requirements, deadline, eligibility and moderation rules before entries open.
  2. Equal entry limits. Judge each project’s best eligible entry for the team result. A bigger member count does not create extra judging slots.
  3. Published scoring. Proposed rubric: originality 40%, execution 30%, usefulness 20%, clarity 10%. Reviewers explain their scores; ties share a place unless final rules publish a different method in advance.
  4. Accountable judging. Confirm reviewers and disclose conflicts before launch. A conflicted reviewer must recuse; use an independent replacement. Publish a correction and dispute process before accepting entries.
  5. Separate audience choice. Optional audience voting would have its own result and eligibility/abuse controls. It would not change the judged champion. No paid or token-weighted votes are proposed.
  6. Show the work. Proposed recognition is a winner showcase and station achievement after results are reviewed and publication consent is confirmed. No cash, token allocation, giveaway or financial benefit is announced.

This page cannot accept a competition entry or enroll a team. The four-project limit and two-week format describe a pilot proposal, not a scheduled season.

How the first season would count members
  1. New eligible accounts, explicit community choice. A recruit must create an eligible Federation account within the season, pass the enrollment checks and choose an approved station. Opening a station link is not membership.
  2. One account, one community per season. Repeat sign-ins, visits and duplicate submissions do not add joins. An account cannot score for several communities in the same season.
  3. Participation is separate from recruitment. Activation needs a server-verified onboarding mission. A return needs activation plus a verified action at least seven days after joining, before season end. Local Passport badges and self-reported screenshots are not League proof.
  4. Counts need review. Test, duplicate and ineligible accounts are excluded. Suspected abuse is held for review. Account verification alone does not establish one unique human; no bot-proof claim is made.
  5. Clear scope. No credit for buying tokens, wallet size, trade volume, clicks or automated posts. No paid prize, token allocation or financial benefit is announced.
  6. Dated results and corrections. Published standings show their capture time and count only approved stations. Final totals and any corrections need review; individual emails, account IDs and wallet addresses are not displayed.

These are the proposed counting rules. The first season needs published dates and final rules, account clearance, verified enrollment and mission events, abuse controls and partner approval before any real score is collected.

Sharing introduces the League. It does not track an invite or count a join.

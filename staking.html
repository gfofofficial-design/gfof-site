GFOF / PROPOSED HOLDER PROGRAM

STAKING PREPARATION.

DESIGN REVIEW · NO POOL OPEN

The Federation plans to offer an optional, funded GFOF token reward pool after the token's Meteora bonding curve completes and migration is verified. The program has no published rate, lock term, reward budget, provider, or opening date.

No wallet action is requested here.

This page never connects to a wallet or takes a deposit. Any future pool link will appear here only after its address, funding transaction, withdrawal rules, participant fees, and risks are published and checked. This status is a preparation record, not an active offer.

What is being designed

01 / Funding

A bounded reward vault

Rewards would come from a defined GFOF allocation placed in a public pool. Holding GFOF outside that pool would earn nothing from it. No new tokens or future revenue stream are presumed available for this plan.

02 / Trigger

Verified migration

The current GFOF launch uses Meteora DBC. Its configured quote-reserve threshold and the resulting DAMM v2 migration must be verified on chain. A market cap figure or calendar date does not trigger this program.

03 / Participation

Clear terms first

Before launch, the Federation will specify the cap, entry period, actual lock and exit rules, fees, reward budget, pool authority and provider. Token pool rewards are distinct from Solana validator staking.

04 / Evidence

Receipts anyone can check

Pool, program and reward vault addresses, funding signatures, current status and any corrections will be linked from the official Federation site. A pool is open only when the evidence card says it is.

What must happen before opening

This is a change from the earlier public “staking not committed” posture. A separate specification, legal review of the actual terms and public correction must precede promotion of any reward terms. No Dossier beta feature or paid access depends on staking.

  1. 01 Verify Meteora DBC completion and DAMM v2 pool from finalized receipts.
  2. 02 Identify an unencumbered token allocation and approve the exact funding budget.
  3. 03 Review the chosen provider, contract, participant fees and withdrawal behavior with counsel and test a small isolated position.
  4. 04 Create and fund the pool, verify its vault and publish a dated terms and evidence record before linking any wallet action.
Risk record.

Token value can fall. Pool software, upgrade authority, funding shortfall, provider availability and lock terms can affect access to tokens or rewards. Specific risks and rules will be published with the chosen pool; no return is promised on this page.

For the current launch state, see the roadmap. The corrections record controls if a previous statement differs from a new approved program.

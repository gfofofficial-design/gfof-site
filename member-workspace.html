Help when you need it.

You can contact GFOF even when the member service is closed or you are signed out. These links open your email app; review and send the message there. Opening a link does not send a report or create a tracked ticket.

  • Get help with a profile or interrupted change

    If a save has an unknown outcome, use its explicit check-or-finish option when available. It may finish the original change. Avoid starting the same change again or assuming cancellation undid it. For help, describe the action, approximate time and visible status in your own words.

  • Report an unwanted interaction

    Describe what happened and what help you need. Include only the minimum relevant details; avoid forwarding a whole conversation or another person’s contact information. A report goes to the GFOF operator for review and does not automatically hide a member, remove content or decide a dispute.

    When available, Hide stops interaction in both directions; unlisting prevents new incoming invitations. Neither sends a report to the operator. Use the separate report link if you want GFOF to review the situation.

  • Account privacy and deletion requests

    Profile removal and account deletion are separate. Removing a profile also affects access to its private collaborations; it does not delete the sign-in account or immediately erase recovery copies. We verify account control before acting on an account-deletion request.

Never send passwords, sign-in codes, recovery links, session tokens, seed phrases or private keys. Nothing in your profile or collaboration draft is attached automatically.

RESPONSIBLE DISCLOSURE · NO PAID BOUNTY

Report Security Issues

The Federation's security posture is a matter of public record. Reports about the website, API, public accounts, published wallets, or other Federation-controlled surfaces are welcome. No paid bounty, token reward, compensation tier, or payment deadline is currently authorized.

DISCLOSURE STATUS

Responsible Disclosure Only

The earlier Red-Team Bounty language is withdrawn. Reports remain welcome, but submitting a report does not create a promise of payment, tokens, reimbursement, or other compensation. See correction #036.

Why disclosure remains open. Phishing, DNS abuse, cloned pages, social engineering, impersonation, credential exposure, and web vulnerabilities can harm users even when no protocol contract is live. Clear reporting channels help the operator investigate and correct verified issues.

No compensation promise. There are no active severity-based reward tiers. The Federation does not promise payment in $GFOF or any other asset, does not lock a USD value when a report is received, and does not promise reimbursement or a donation. Any future paid program would require a separate written policy and recorded authorization before it is announced.

PROGRAM SCOPE

In Scope / Out of Scope

✓ IN SCOPE
galacticfederation.co · all subdomains · the /app widget · the /treasury page · Netlify functions at /api/* · @GFOF_Offcial on X · t.me/GFOF_SOL · the four active Streamflow lock contracts and preserved predecessor contract · any operational wallet published on /treasury · any federation-owned service disclosed publicly.
✗ OUT OF SCOPE
Third-party services (Solana RPC endpoints, DexScreener, Streamflow itself, Raydium, Moonshot) — report to their own programs. Attacks requiring privileged physical access to a team member's hardware. DDoS or rate-limit attacks. Vulnerabilities requiring severely outdated or unpatched user software. Any test that requires unauthorized access to other users' wallets, funds, or personal data.
✓ RULES OF ENGAGEMENT
Do not exploit a finding beyond what is necessary to document it safely. Do not access, modify, or destroy data belonging to another person. Do not use a finding to extort, blackmail, or harm the Federation or its community. This page does not authorize access, testing, or conduct that would otherwise be unlawful.
✗ NOT ACTIONABLE
Theoretical findings without proof of exploitability. Duplicates of already-reported issues. Self-XSS. Missing security headers without demonstrated impact. Reports generated solely by automated scanners with no validation. Any report from a researcher under 18 or from a jurisdiction sanctioned by the US, UK, or EU.
REPORT A FINDING

Responsible Disclosure

Send enough detail to reproduce and assess the issue. No response, remediation, publication, or payment deadline is promised; the sole operator will prioritize reports by apparent user risk.

Step 1. Draft a clear writeup. Include: (a) the vulnerability class, (b) safe reproduction steps, (c) evidence of impact, (d) your suggested severity, and (e) whether you want public credit.

Step 2. Send it via DM to @WelksCrypto on X or to the Telegram admin of t.me/GFOF_SOL. Do not send private keys, seed phrases, credentials, personal data, or live exploit material through an unencrypted channel; first request coordination for a safer transfer method.

Step 3. Do not publicly disclose the finding until we confirm remediation. If we do not respond within 7 days, you are free to escalate by publishing a non-exploitable summary and requesting public response.

Step 4. If the issue is verified and remediated, public credit may be offered with your permission. No compensation is promised or authorized.

01 / BUILDERS

Review a mechanism

Examine a design assumption, model a failure scenario, or propose a test that could improve the specification.

Start hereBorrower-protective liquidation →Open questions include oracle outages, grace periods, and liquidator incentives.

Prepare a technical review

Use these headings to frame your contribution:

  1. Reference: specification version and section.
  2. Scenario: the conditions or assumptions you are testing.
  3. Finding: expected behavior, failure mode, and supporting calculation or evidence.
  4. Next step: a proposed change or test, including its trade-offs.

02 / BUSINESSES

Bring a real use case

Describe a financial workflow that is difficult today and what moving it on-chain would need to improve.

Start hereCurrent scope and development gates →Use the published stages to frame an exploratory conversation.

Prepare a use-case brief

A useful brief can be just four points:

  1. Workflow: who needs to do what, and where the process breaks down.
  2. Outcome: what a better result would look like and how you would measure it.
  3. Constraints: privacy, permissions, settlement, reporting, or integration needs.
  4. Question: which Federation project relates to the problem and what you want to explore.

03 / RESEARCHERS

Challenge the evidence

Test whether a public claim is supported. Bring a reproducible observation, a counterexample, or a better way to measure it.

Start hereThe Federation's public record →Read the original claim alongside its sources and recorded corrections.

Prepare an evidence review

Make the observation easy for someone else to check:

  1. Claim: quote the relevant passage and link to its source.
  2. Evidence: identify the data, date, method, and limitations.
  3. Result: explain what supports or contradicts the claim.
  4. Reproduction: provide the steps or public references needed to check it.

Start the conversation

# Proposed corrections entry — staking intent (review draft)

Date to publish: **the date this change is approved and published**, not the drafting date.

Earlier public statements at `/roadmap` and in the access design said staking and rewards were uncommitted, and the access design explicitly excluded staking for yield from its scope. Those statements accurately described the policy at the time. The Federation has now decided to prepare a separate optional GFOF reward-pool program for after verified Meteora DBC migration.

This decision changes the earlier **project-wide “not committed” posture** to **“planned, with terms and funding pending.”** It does not amend the optional Dossier payment mechanism or activate a pool. No APY, reward budget, lock term, funding source, provider, start date or distribution is approved or live. The prior creator-fee Moonshot/Raydium specification remains superseded and cannot be treated as a source of staking rewards. The current DBC migration is governed by its configured quote-reserve threshold and resulting DAMM v2 state.

Before opening, publish a separately reviewed staking specification, the source and amount of unencumbered reward tokens, contract and pool addresses, terms, fees, risks and funding receipts. Obtain counsel review of the actual issuer-funded reward arrangement and public copy. Correct any further historical page that states a project-wide no-staking commitment; preserve dated prior claims in the correction history.

Proposed public summary: **“We plan to offer optional GFOF token staking after verified Meteora migration. The pool is not open. Its provider, funded budget, rate, lock and withdrawal rules, fees, risks and opening date will be published before participation. This would be an issuer-funded token reward pool, not Solana validator staking.”**

This is a draft. It should enter the append-only corrections record only with the owner-approved final wording and date. Do not edit the historical entry in place.

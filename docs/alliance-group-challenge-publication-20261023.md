# Small-group challenge publication

The public board can represent one reviewed challenge with two to four project
teams, one rules page and one shared opening/closing window. A four-project
creative pilot does not need invented pairings or separate match records.
The shipping challenge and partner arrays remain empty. This source capability
does not open entries, confirm teams, publish rules or establish a real event.

## Record the agreement before publication

Every participating team must already be a source-reviewed published station.
Keep its authorized representative, exact agreed rules version and acceptance
evidence in the restricted operator record. A submitted introduction, sign-in,
team mention or self-described affiliation does not supply that agreement.

For a new challenge, `review.acceptedTeams` contains exactly the participating
public station IDs, each once. Its order does not matter. The validator refuses
missing, duplicate or additional IDs, unknown/unapproved stations and more than
four participants. The other review fields remain `publication`, `reviewedAt`
and `publicReference`, with the existing approval and date requirements.

The original `bothTeamsAccepted: true` review shape is retained for existing
two-team records only. It cannot approve a three- or four-team challenge. Do not
combine both review shapes. These fields are operator attestations, not an
authentication service or independent verification of consent. All source is
downloadable; never add private contacts, conversations or agreement contents.

## Publish and track one shared event

After participants approve the same final brief and publication scope, prepare
the numbered rules page and review it with the challenge record. Use one
`ARENA-NNNN` identity and its matching local rules path. The static build admits
only numbered rules/results pages; a record must not point to a missing page.
Agree and test the actual entry route, support, retention, moderation, judges,
disputes and cancellation process before announcing dates or opening entries.

Retain entry IDs, eligibility, scores, disputes and dated changes privately.
The board publishes reviewed status and agreed UTC dates, not automatic live
scores or an inferred event state. An entry tracker and scoring service are
separate work. Publishing a group record does not activate those services.

Final results still require the matching reviewed results page and a record
update no earlier than closure. Preserve corrections and superseded evidence.
The public projection contains team identities but omits acceptance metadata.
No prize, financial entitlement or holder ranking is introduced.
